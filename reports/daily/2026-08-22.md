- **[你当主角](https://skillhub.cn/skills/you-as-protagonist)** `精选` 安装量 0
   Generate short films where the user becomes the protagonist of their favorite movies. This skill is triggered when a user provides (1) a personal photo, (2) a nickname or character name, and (3) the name of a movie plus a description of the iconic scene to remake. It produces a 10–30 second short video that recreates the cinematic atmosphere of the referenced film with the user as the leading character, by orchestrating ImageGen (image-to-image keyframes), VideoGen (image-to-video clips), FFmpeg-based stitching with burned-in subtitles, crossfade/flash transitions, and cue-aligned BGM (auto-detect BGM beats and snap shot transitions to strong beats).
